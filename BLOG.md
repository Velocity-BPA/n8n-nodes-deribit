# Automate Your Deribit Crypto Trading with Our New n8n Community Node

We're excited to announce the release of our latest n8n community node: n8n-nodes-deribit! Created by Velocity BPA, this powerful integration brings automated cryptocurrency derivatives trading to your n8n workflows.

## The Challenge of Crypto Derivatives Trading

Trading options, futures, and perpetual contracts on Deribit requires constant monitoring, quick decision-making, and repetitive tasks. Whether you're managing risk, executing complex trading strategies, or simply tracking your portfolio, manual processes can lead to missed opportunities and increased exposure.

Traders and crypto businesses need a way to automate their Deribit operations without building custom integrations from scratch. That's where our new node comes in.

## What Can You Do with n8n-nodes-deribit?

This community node enables seamless integration between Deribit's cryptocurrency derivatives platform and your n8n automation workflows. Here's what you can accomplish:

- **Automated Trading**: Execute trades based on custom triggers, market conditions, or signals from other platforms
- **Portfolio Management**: Monitor positions, track P&L, and manage risk automatically
- **Alert Systems**: Set up sophisticated notifications for price movements, margin levels, or order fills
- **Data Collection**: Pull historical data, order books, and trade information for analysis
- **Multi-Platform Strategies**: Combine Deribit data with other exchanges, analytics tools, or communication platforms

The node supports Deribit's full API capabilities for options, futures, and perpetual contracts, giving you complete control over your automated trading strategies.

## Getting Started

Installing the Deribit node is simple. In your n8n instance, run:


npm install n8n-nodes-deribit


After installation, restart n8n, and you'll find the Deribit node available in your node palette. Connect it to your Deribit account using API credentials, and you're ready to start automating.

For detailed documentation and code, check out our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Velocity-BPA/n8n-nodes-deribit).
